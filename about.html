About

Swastik P. — Founder, The Kreator Syndicate

I've been in the digital info space since I was 14.

It wasn't planned. It was survival.

Money was always a problem growing up. When I hit high school, I started looking into the online money-making space seriously. Before that, I'd made some money combining programming and Minecraft by selling mods — but I lost basically all of it when it was stored in crypto. Lesson learned the hard way.

Around 15, I started binging business content. Around the same time, I met one of my mentors. He was an online coach making real money at 19. That's where I learned how the coaching space actually works — not the theory, the mechanics.

There, I met a few aspiring coaches who became close friends. We'd hop on calls day and night. I helped them start their businesses and make their first sales. They were the ones who told me I had a real knack for scaling online info businesses. That I should look into it more seriously.

So I did. I worked 1:1 with coaches, helped them grow. I spent most of my time learning everything about this space — so much so that I went from the top of my class in high school to almost failing at one point. The obsession came with a cost.

Now that I've graduated, after 3+ years of learning and doing, I feel competent enough to deliver real results. And I'm building The Kreator Syndicate to do it at scale.

This isn't a course. It's not consulting. It's a system — and I've spent years building it.

Why The Kreator Syndicate exists

Most info-product businesses fail for the same reason: they're built around a person, not a system.

I've seen it happen enough times to know there's a better way. The businesses that scale past the $50k a month mark all share one thing: they have infrastructure. Systems for acquisition, conversion, delivery, and retention. Systems that run without the founder in every single piece.

That's what we build here. Eight operators at a time. With full attention. With a proven playbook. With someone who's been in the trenches and knows exactly what separates the ones that scale from the ones that stall.
