## The system works in three phases

### Phase 1: The Signal Protocol

**Months 1–2.**
The goal is simple: a validated offer, your first funnel, and revenue on the board.

We start with a real business conversation.
Not a generic form.
Not a surface-level audit.
A proper look at where you are, what is working, what is slowing you down, and what the fastest path to leverage looks like in your situation.

Then we run your offer through the **Offer-Lock System™**.

Most creators build offers they like.
We build offers the market already wants.

We look at the language your buyers use.
We look at the gap your offer fills.
We sharpen the positioning until the offer becomes easy to understand, easy to buy, and easy to sell at a premium price.

Once that is clear, we build the acquisition funnel and run your first launch.
Not a huge production.
A controlled test.
One that is designed to get your first paying clients, prove the offer, and show us what needs to be improved next.

By the end of Phase 1, you have a validated offer, first revenue, and a working acquisition system.

### Phase 2: The Revenue Foundation

**Months 3–6.**
The goal is predictable income that does not reset every month.

Most info-product businesses live in launch mode forever.
A spike.
A slowdown.
Another launch.
Another slowdown.

Phase 2 is where that pattern starts to break.

We run your business through the **Constraint Finder™**.

That tells us where the real bottleneck is.
Is it leads?
Is it conversion?
Is it retention?
Is it delivery capacity?
Most people keep fixing the wrong thing. This makes the right problem obvious.

Then we install the right system based on what is actually holding you back.

- If the problem is leads, we build the **Signalling Engine™** — content that attracts buyers, not just followers.
- If the problem is conversion, we redesign your sales process with the **Frictionless Close Framework™** — something structured, repeatable, and easier to run consistently.
- If the problem is retention and delivery, we shape your program so clients get results faster and stay longer without needing more of you.

We also build your metrics system.
Because you cannot scale what you cannot see.

By the end of Phase 2, you know your lead volume, your conversion rate, your client lifetime value, and which lever to pull next.
